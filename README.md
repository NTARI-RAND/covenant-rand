# covenant-rand

Research & Development on the Covenant Layer of the Janus Facing Architecture.

A social contract written in code: how prosumers rate their exchanges and what those ratings mean. Leaving is dear, so members vote on changes.

Research is discussed in #rand-covenant and voted on in #federation-covenant in the NTARI Slack workspace.

## Structure

| Tier | Goal |
|---|---|
| [Tier 1: The Leveson-Based Trade Assessment Scale](tier-1-lbtas/) | Understand how a promise not to harm becomes a rating |
| [Tier 2: The Covenant API](tier-2-covenant-api/) | Understand how ratings are served across many markets without becoming one score |
| [Tier 3: Rating and Adjudication in the Interface](tier-3-rating-and-adjudication/) | Implement the covenant in the interfaces where people rate each other and settle disputes |

## How we research

1. Fork this repository.
2. Add your file to the correct tier folder.
3. Create a pull request with a [DCO signature](https://github.com/apps/dco).
4. Wait for peer review.

#federation-covenant has 90 days to deliberate your publication, during which you defend your claims and make the changes reviewers ask for. Follow the [Research & Development Submission Guide](https://ntari.slack.com/docs/T0978JCFFSL/F0BTB5N93A9): the method, the ethics check and the submission template.

Merged research is published in #federation-volunteers, on [www.ntari.org](https://www.ntari.org) and affiliated social media channels, with the author(s) credited.
